Audio design

Making sound for games

I'm the Lead Audio Designer on Project Gravity, a two-year game we're making at Cosmic Labs with an 18-person team. Below is some of the sound library I've been building for it, plus a demo I made of reactive, spatial audio in Unreal Engine.

01Gravity SFX library

  • Lead Audio Designer
  • Team of 18
  • Ableton · Output plugins
  • Jira · Perforce

These are upcoming sound effects for Gravity. Many are still in ideation, and they've helped our team define its mood board and the kinds of sounds and light motifs we want in the game. The gravity "gun" sound is central to the game, so I'm still workshopping it to get it exactly right. Everything was made in Ableton, mostly with built-in synths plus a few Output plugins.

02Reactive audio in Unreal

  • Unreal Engine
  • Blueprints · MetaSounds

In this demo I was experimenting with reactive and spatial audio in Unreal Engine. I used animation notifies in the character Blueprint to trigger footsteps at precise moments in the walk cycle, then built a Blueprint system that picks different footstep sounds based on the physical material underfoot.

For the environment, I set up sound attenuation so audio behaves realistically in 3D space. Using MetaSounds, I designed a fluorescent light with a subtle electrical hum that fades with the player's distance, and gave the water outside a similar location-based presence instead of playing it globally.

It's animation, Blueprint logic, physical materials, attenuation, and MetaSounds all working together in one scene.
