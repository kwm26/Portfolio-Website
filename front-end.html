Product Design · Athletic Training Portal

Making movement data useful for coaches and athletes.

ATP turns regular phone video into movement data. I designed and built our landing page, the athlete mobile view, and the overlays that draw that data onto video.

Tools

The problem

One set of data, very different users. Young athletes want something engaging on their phone. Coaches need the full picture but don't always love technology.

Video to skeleton

Raw video next to the tracked skeleton, so people can see the tracking is right before they trust the numbers.

Design decisions

Coaches see who needs attention first

Every number sits against a healthy range, in plain language

Athletes get one score and a body they can tap

Screens

Real-time overlays

I built the overlay system in Python with OpenCV. It started at 7 FPS. I got it past 60.

Results

$50K grant and NCAA Division I pilots at Duke and North Carolina Central University.
