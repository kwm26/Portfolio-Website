Games I've worked on

Mostly team projects in Unity, where I worked on mechanics, level systems, sound, and 3D models. There's also an original concept of mine that made the finals of a pitch competition. My sound work has its own page: Audio Design.

01Duo Chrome

  • Team of 5
  • 3 weeks
  • Unity · version control

We built Duo Chrome as a team of five in three weeks. It was a good test of working in Unity and using version control with a team.

My part: I built the dash mechanic, the checkpoint and level-transition systems, and the first level after the tutorial.

Play on itch.ioGitHub

02Snowventure

  • Team of 6
  • 4 weeks
  • 3D puzzle-platformer · FMOD

Snowventure was the same team plus one more programmer, over four weeks. It was my first time working on a fully 3D puzzle-platformer.

My part: I made and integrated the music and sound effects, mostly in FMOD. I also modeled and programmed the checkpoint flag and modeled other objects in the game, like the lamp post shown here.

GitHub

03Minimal Shade

  • Original concept
  • Pitch finalist · 6 of 40

Minimal Shade is an original concept I've only had the chance to rough-prototype, but it's one I'm really passionate about. I pitched it in a month-long game pitch contest and made the final round, 6 out of 40 pitches. It wasn't one of the three winners, but it shows how I think about original ideas that push against the norms of the genre.
